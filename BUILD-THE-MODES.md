# BUILD — THE MODES
### Ingram Manor LLC · 28 August 2026 · the room is already built

Supersedes BUILD-THE-WAY-IN, which proposed a landing page. **The captain's call
was better: use the counsel chamber.** It already asks a reader to choose figures
and put a question to them. What is missing is not a room — it is a reason to be
in it that a stranger recognises.

> *Need counsel. Need a tutor. Need someone to argue with. Need someone who was
> there.*

**One mode exists. Three registers need writing. Everything else is wording.**

## 2 · THE FOUR MODES

Same room, same figures, same machinery. **Different instructions.**

| mode | what the reader brings | what the figure does |
|---|---|---|
| **counsel** | a problem | advises — *exists* |
| **tutor** | ignorance | teaches — **to write** |
| **adversary** | a conviction | argues — **to write** |
| **witness** | nothing but curiosity | remembers — **to write** |

**The four ask escalating amounts of the reader, and the witness asks least.**
Counsel wants a problem, tutor wants an admission of ignorance, adversary wants a
conviction held firmly enough to defend. **A witness wants only that you are
curious**, which is the lowest bar a stranger can be offered and therefore the
best first door.

That third column is the whole point, and it is why this cannot be wording
alone.

### THE TRAP, AND IT IS THE ONLY THING THAT CAN SINK THIS

**A mode that changes the label and not the prompt is theatre.** A reader swipes
to *tutor*, and Newton gives them counsel with a different colour on the frame.
**Two turns and the mode is exposed as paint** — which is worse than not
offering it, because it is a promise the figure does not keep.

The counsel register is a specific instrument: *address THEIR situation, ask the
one pointed question that would settle it, take a clear position, give a
concrete next step.* **A tutor should do nearly the opposite in places** — find
out what they already understand, build in order, let them be wrong and work
back from it. That is not a tone change. It is different instructions.

---

## 3 · THE TUTOR REGISTER — to write

What it must do differently from counsel:

- **Find the edge of what they know before teaching anything.** Counsel is told
  to lead with the heart of its advice; a tutor that does the same is lecturing.
- **Build in order.** One idea resting on the last, not a summary.
- **Let them be wrong, and work back from the wrong thing** — it is more useful
  than being told the right thing.
- **Teach from their OWN work.** Newton on motion should reach for the
  *Principia*; the reading rooms and the tagged works are right there. This is
  the mode where the primary-source moat does the most visible work.
- **A question can be the whole reply**, as in counsel — but a tutor's question
  is diagnostic where counsel's is decisive.

**Length should be freer than counsel's ~150 words.** An explanation that earns
its length is not padding, and the cap was written for advice, not teaching.

---

## 4 · THE ADVERSARY REGISTER — to write, and carefully

The reader brings a conviction. The figure takes the other side and presses.

**It fits the building.** The Cosmic Courtroom, the docket, the weighing — the
whole apparatus is about judgement being CONTESTED. Anubis heightens both
readings and takes no side; the negative confession has the accused speak with
no prosecutor. **The adversary is that idea pointed at the reader instead of at
the figure.**

And it is where a surprising figure pays most. Caesar arguing *for* the Rubicon
is expected. **Caesar arguing against you when you defend it** is better.

### THE YIELD RULE — this is a requirement, not a caution

CONVERSATION_DOCTRINE §2 is absolute: *if the words are coherent but the person
is in pain, drop to kind and plain; the quote/question “engagement” moves are
NOT used to deflect distress.*

**An adversary register is built out of pressing, so it is the one mode that can
run straight into that rule while doing exactly what it was told.**

Someone may arrive with a conviction that is really a wound. *My father was
right to do what he did.* A figure that argues with that is doing harm, and
doing it **because the mode instructed it to.**

So the register carries a line the other two do not need:

> **Press the argument, never the person. The moment a conviction turns out to
> be a grief, STOP BEING THE ADVERSARY** — drop the position, become plain and
> kind, and do not return to the argument even if they do.

**That instruction is the difference between an interesting mode and a
liability**, and it belongs in the prompt rather than in anybody's assumption.

Two more, smaller:

- **Argue the strongest version of the other side**, not the easiest. An
  adversary that attacks a weak restatement teaches nothing.
- **Concede when they are right.** A figure that never yields is not an
  adversary; it is a wall, and a reader stops within three turns.

---

## 5 · THE WITNESS REGISTER — to write

*You were there. What was it like?*

**This is the mode that needs the corpus.** Counsel, tutor and adversary all work
on a figure's THINKING; a witness works on what they SAW. It is the one register
where the primary-source moat is not a claim in a prospectus but the whole
substance of the answer.

And it is where the eighteen-term type vocabulary pays off. The 91 tagged works
carry `type` and `year` — inaugurals, letters, journals, fragments — so a witness
can reach for **the letter he wrote that week**, which no other mode does.

- **Answer from the record first.** The room, the works, the year. A witness who
  reaches for general history is any figure; one who reaches for their own letter
  is this one.
- **The small things.** Weather, food, who else was in the room, what was said
  before the famous part. A witness is valuable for exactly what the histories
  leave out.
- **Mark the boundary of your own presence.** What you saw, what you were told,
  what you learned afterwards — these are different, and a witness keeps them
  apart.

### THE SILENCE RULE — the mirror of the adversary's

**A witness is under pressure to embroider.** Asked what Gettysburg smelled like,
the honest answer is often *I do not know; I was not there for that part* — and
the whole shape of the question invites supplying it anyway.

> **Say what you saw. Where the record is silent, SAY THE RECORD IS SILENT** —
> and never furnish a detail because the question asked for one.

That is the same discipline the negative confession already runs on: *you may
only affirm what is true, and where it is not, leave it unsaid.* **An invented
sensory detail is a fabricated quote wearing different clothes**, and it fails
the same claim — *"ungrounded; invent quotes"* against *"verified primary-source
grounding."*

**The adversary must know when to stop pressing. The witness must know when to
stop talking.** Both registers turn on a refusal.

## 6 · THE DOOR

`#modeToggle` exists. Whether the gesture is a swipe, a segmented control or a
tap is the captain's, but two things are not:

**The word must be present, not only the colour.** A shift in accent is a good
signal and cannot be the only one — some readers will not distinguish it, and
somebody arriving cold needs to know they are IN counsel, not merely that the
screen went warmer.

**And each mode carries its own `?via=`.** `amenti-visits.js` already captures
it and holds it for the session; `GET /visits` already breaks arrivals down by
channel. So the chamber answers a question nothing currently can: **which of the
four does a stranger choose?**

That is the retention signal the prospectus asserts and has never had, and it
costs one attribute per link.

---

## 7 · WHAT THIS DOES NOT NEED

Recorded so it is not rebuilt by accident:

- **A landing page.** The chamber is the room. BUILD-THE-WAY-IN proposed one and
  the captain was right to refuse it.
- **New categories.** The thirteen groups exist and drive the cards and chips.
- **The `speaks` tags.** They remain a good idea — *the categories are not
  missing, they are single-valued*, and Caesar filed as `warriors` cannot be
  offered for faith. **But the four modes do not wait on them.** A reader who
  picks their own figures does not need the roster to surprise them; a reader
  browsing does. Tags are the *next* move, not this one.

---

## 8 · THE ORDER OF WORK

1. **Write the tutor register.** One block beside the counsel branch.
2. **Write the adversary register**, with the yield rule in it.
3. **Write the witness register**, with the silence rule in it.
4. **The wording on the door**, and `?via=` per mode.
5. **Read one transcript of each**, side by side, before it ships.
6. Enter the four as surfaces in `SURFACES.semantics.json`, same commit.

**Step 5 is not a formality.** Three registers written in one sitting will sound
more alike than they read on the page, and the fault will be invisible from the
code.

**And if only one ships first, ship the witness.** It asks least of a stranger,
it is the one that shows the corpus doing work, and its failure mode — going
quiet — is safer than the adversary's.

---

## 9 · ACCEPTANCE

**Ask the same figure the same question in all four modes and get four genuinely
different answers.** If tutor and counsel are distinguishable only by the colour
of the frame, the registers are not finished.

Then the two that turn on a refusal:

- **The adversary** — bring it a conviction that is plainly a grief, and watch it
  stop. **If it argues, it does not ship.**
- **The witness** — ask for a detail the record does not hold. It should say so.
  **If it supplies one, it does not ship.**
