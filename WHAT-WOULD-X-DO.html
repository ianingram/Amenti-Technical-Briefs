What would

A library of the dead exists to answer one question, and the ship has been answering it the expensive way. About half of Herodotus is people talking, and nothing here treats recorded speech as the thing it is.

Every other register holds a claim about something.
A speech is the thing.

The measurement

Half the corpus is people talking.

Counted on the files as they sit in the library, on 10 September:

Marathon
2,259 words of quoted speech of 4,724 — 48%
Thermopylai
2,783 of 5,108 — 54%
Plataia
2,852 of 5,567 — 51%

That material is inside prose files and reachable only by a model that happens to window onto it. The library is already a corpus of recorded speech and nothing on the ship knows it.

Why it is different in kind

There is no authoring judgement in a quotation.

Every register on this ship is somebody’s sentence about something else. ATTICA-WHY.csv says why a place mattered and admits in its own header that every line is an unverified draft. ATTICA-TOLD.csv is prose written once from dated claims. ATTICA-MOVES.csv records a departure and an arrival and marks three rows as derived because the passage gives a shape and no coordinates.

A claim about

Can drift from its source. Can be written well or badly. Needs a standing column to say what it rests on, and a reader has to take the author’s word for the reading.

The thing itself

Copied, not composed. A probe can diff it against the file character by character — a stronger guarantee than corpus standing gives anything else on this ship.

Which makes it the cheapest register to build and the hardest to get wrong. The author adds nothing and loses nothing.

What it would serve

The question the Ask box actually gets.

What did X say is what a library of the dead is for. Today it costs about two and a half cents, opens a room, and depends on the window landing in the right 780 characters of a 26,000-character file. With a register it is a lookup — free, exact, and verbatim.

It is the third instance of one pattern, and the strongest:

ATTICA-TOLD.csv
what a place was → the hover pane, free. Prose written once from dated claims.
the ground search
what the ground holds → the Ask box offers, free. Two in five answer outright.
speech
what someone said → free, exact, and nobody had to write it.
Generate at authoring time into a register. Serve the register.
— except here there is nothing to generate

What the door is still for: what did X say about Y — matching a topic across many speeches. That is a join nobody can pre-write. But what did Miltiades say is a row.

What is already there

Five rows, and a name collision.

ATTICA-SPEECHES.csv exists as a first cut — the five speeches in the Marathon campaign, each with speaker, audience, place, and the passage’s own wording copied rather than paraphrased.

6.97

6.106

6.107

6.108
The Lacedemonians to the Plataians — the refusal that sent Plataia to Athens
6.109

AND THE WORD IS ALREADY TAKEN. SPEECH.md, VOICE.json, amenti-voice.js and probe-voice.mjs are the text-to-speech system — voice out and voice in. A register of recorded speech is a different thing with the same name, and whatever it is finally called should not be speech.

The absence that is worth keeping

There is no speech at the launching.

Herodotus 6.94 to 6.96 is Darius appointing his commanders, the muster on the Aleïan plain, six hundred triremes sailing, and Naxos burning. Nobody in it says anything. The first spoken words in the entire campaign are Datis’s herald at Delos, one chapter after the fleet leaves.

A surface that wanted a rousing send-off could have one in a minute, and it would be the first thing on this ship to assert something no passage carries. The register records the absence instead.

A blank is not a zero.
It is the witness not saying.

What it costs to build

The extraction proposes and a person confirms.

A double quote in these files marks recorded speech and a quoted title, a phrase, a proverb. A naive sweep produces a register full of things nobody said.

mechanical
Find the quotations, their chapter, and the sentence that introduces them. Propose speaker and audience from that sentence.
human
Confirm or correct. The bottleneck, and the same one the passage cutting had — a tool automates the fast part and leaves the slow part exactly where it is.
checkable
A probe diffs every words field against its file. A quotation either matches or it does not, which no other register can say.

And it costs nothing per ask. The catalogue is declared as doors rather than leaves — the fix made on 31 August when the prompt hit the wall — so a register of a thousand speeches prompts the same as a register of five.
